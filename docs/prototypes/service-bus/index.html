Service Bus — UX layout prototypes

Three directions for decluttering the feature. Click a card to open the interactive mock. Nothing here is wired — evaluate the shape, not the pixels.

A — Zones structured regions

Three labelled regions — Navigate / Browse / Inspect — each owning its tools. The global toolbar disappears; actions live in the zone they act on. Detail is a permanent docked zone, not an overlay.

B — Ribbon Office-style tabs

All ~20 commands organized into 4 ribbon tabs (Home / Messages / DLQ & Recovery / View) with labelled groups and big primary buttons. Collapses to a tab strip. Destructive ops get their own visually-separated group.

C — Progressive disclosure hide by default

One line of chrome: breadcrumb, counts, Active/DLQ, Compose, ⋯. Entities, filters, view prefs and batch ops live in on-demand overlays. Hover reveals per-row actions; a selected message opens a bottom drawer.
